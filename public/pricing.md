# Andishi Pricing Spec

Andishi operates on transparent scoping. We don't hide costs behind account manager layers. Every project receives a direct scope, timeline, and cost breakdown in a one-page brief after a scoping call.

## Product Build Pricing (Project-Based)

Product builds are structured in weekly sprint deliverables. Below are indicative ranges based on past delivered work:

| Service Category | Timeline | Budget Range (USD) | Core Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Web Applications** | 4–10 weeks | $5,000 – $15,000 | Responsive Custom Web App, Database, Payments |
| **SaaS Platforms** | 6–14 weeks | $8,000 – $22,000 | Multi-tenant SaaS MVP, Subscription, Admin Panel |
| **Mobile Applications** | 6–12 weeks | $7,000 – $18,000 | iOS & Android Apps, App Store Publishing |
| **AI & Intelligent Systems** | 3–8 weeks | $5,000 – $12,000 | LLM Integrations, RAG pipelines, Agentic workflows |
| **APIs & Integrations** | 2–6 weeks | $3,000 – $8,000 | Custom API, M-Pesa/Stripe Integration, Pipelines |
| **Enterprise Software** | 8–20 weeks | Custom Quote | Internal Workflow Engine, Legacy Overhaul |
| **Product Strategy & UX** | 1–3 weeks | $1,500 – $4,000 | Interactive Figma Mockups, Technical Scoping Brief |

## Engineering Staff Augmentation (Embedded Monthly)

For teams that want to embed senior developers directly into their existing sprint cycles:

| Role Level | Experience Level | Monthly Rate (USD) | Engagement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Senior Engineer** | 5+ Years | $5,000 – $9,000 / mo | Embedded, timezone-aligned |
| **Lead Engineer** | 8+ Years | $8,000 – $14,000 / mo | Technical setup, mentoring, delivery |

*All augmentation placements include a 30-day replacement guarantee and continuous onboarding check-ins.*

## Start a Scoping Brief
To get an exact scope and quote:
1. Submit your project requirements at [andishi.dev/start-project](https://andishi.dev/start-project) (takes 2 minutes).
2. Schedule a 20-minute scoping call.
3. Receive a one-page fixed-price brief within 48 hours of the call.
